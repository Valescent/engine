# v0.0.6 scope: Atmosphere

**Status:** In progress - slices 1-2 complete.

**Goal:** Make the world readable and atmospheric with lighting, a day/night cycle, fog, and water.

This page expands the [v0.0.6 roadmap entry](../roadmap.md#v006-atmosphere) into concrete deliverables. The roadmap remains the source of truth for milestone status and the Definition of Done.

See [v0.0.6 spec](../specs/v0.0.6.md) for the normative lighting, AO, day/night, fog, water, and `VCHK` v2 contracts.

## Definition of Done

- Sky light and block light propagate through the world and look correct in caves and at the surface.
- Ambient occlusion is visible in corners and crevices.
- A day/night cycle changes the sun direction, ambient light, and sky colour.
- Distance fog hides the render-distance edge.
- Water is a distinct transparent, animated surface.

## In scope

- 4-bit sky light and block light per voxel.
- Flood-fill propagation for both light types.
- A solid `Lamp` block that emits block light.
- Ambient occlusion baked into chunk vertices.
- Directional sun/moon lighting and ambient light.
- A ten-minute day/night cycle with a debug time control.
- Distance fog tied to the render distance.
- A non-solid `Water` block rendered in a separate transparent layer.
- Animated water normals.
- CPU-only tests for light propagation, AO, time-of-day, and water classification.

## Out of scope

- Shadow maps and real-time global illumination. See [ADR-0011](../decisions/0011-voxel-light-and-baked-ao.md) for the voxel-light/AO decision.
- Volumetric fog, weather, and clouds.
- Water reflections, refractions, swimming, and flow simulation.
- Coloured light sources.
- Procedural terrain and streaming (v0.0.7).

## Technical shape

### Lighting storage

- Each chunk stores `sky_light` and `block_light` arrays of one byte per local voxel.
- Values are `0..=15`; `15` is full daylight or a lamp at the source.
- Light is derived state: do not save it. Recompute after loading a chunk.
- Use a packed representation only if profiling shows the two arrays are too large.

### Sky and block light

- Sky light starts at `15` in open columns and spreads horizontally with one level of attenuation per step.
- Block light starts at `14` from `Lamp` blocks and spreads through non-solid neighbours.
- Blocks attenuate light; `Air` and `Water` do not block it.
- Propagate with a BFS queue over the affected chunk and its neighbours.
- On an edit, recompute the affected region and invalidate the light and mesh of every dirty chunk.
- Use the v0.0.5 worker pipeline for lighting and remeshing; keep the main thread free.

### Ambient occlusion

- Compute AO per vertex from the three solid neighbours around each face corner.
- Map the result to four levels and bake it into the vertex.
- Include AO in the greedy mesher output, not a separate post-process.

### Vertex and shader changes

- Extend the vertex with texture layer, packed sky/block light, and AO.
- Sample the v0.0.4 texture array using the per-quad layer.
- Combine texture colour, AO, block light, sky light, and directional sun/moon light.
- Keep the shader-based face shading as a fallback for unlit debug scenes.

### Day/night and fog

- Add a time-of-day uniform in `[0, 1)`; a full cycle lasts ten minutes.
- Rotate the sun direction around the world; the moon uses the opposite direction.
- Blend ambient and sky colours through dawn, day, dusk, and night.
- `T` advances the clock by one hour for debugging.
- Fog uses the camera distance and render distance; its colour matches the sky.
- Keep the cycle length and debug key documented in the README.

### Water

- Add a non-solid `Water` block.
- Build a separate transparent mesh per chunk; water-water faces are culled.
- Render transparent meshes after opaque meshes with depth testing on and depth writes off.
- Use alpha blending without per-face sorting; document the ordering limitation.
- Animate normals in the fragment shader using the time uniform.
- Do not add swimming or water collision in this milestone.

### Code shape

- `src/light.rs` — sky/block light propagation and AO helpers.
- `src/voxel.rs` — light-aware chunk meshing and transparent mesh generation.
- `src/renderer.rs` — light/AO vertex layout, day/night uniforms, and the transparent pass.
- `src/shader.wgsl` — lighting, fog, and water animation.
- `src/block.rs` — `Lamp` and `Water` variants and texture layers.
- `src/lib.rs` — time-of-day updates and dirty-chunk integration.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Open surface blocks are fully lit; deep caves are dark.
- [ ] A lamp lights nearby blocks and fades with distance.
- [ ] Breaking a block updates light and meshes without a frame hitch.
- [ ] AO is visible under overhangs and in corners.
- [ ] The day/night cycle changes sun direction, ambient colour, and sky colour.
- [ ] `T` advances the clock by one hour.
- [ ] Fog hides the render-distance edge without swallowing nearby terrain.
- [ ] Water is transparent, visibly animated, and does not write depth.
- [ ] Loading a saved world recomputes lighting correctly.
- [ ] `cargo fmt --all -- --check`, `cargo clippy --all-targets --all-features -- -D warnings`, `cargo test --all-features`, `cargo doc --no-deps --all-features`, `typos`, and `mdbook build` pass.
- [ ] The README and roadmap are current and `CHANGELOG.md` has the user-visible entries.

## Deliberate simplifications

| Simplification | Revisit when |
|---|---|
| Ambient occlusion instead of shadow maps | Shadows are worth the extra passes and artefacts. |
| 4-bit lighting and no coloured light | Coloured or RGB lighting becomes a requirement. |
| Light is recomputed, never saved | Load times or large worlds make recomputation expensive. |
| Water without sorting, reflections, or refraction | Visual quality or transparency artefacts justify the cost. |
| Water does not affect movement | Swimming arrives in a later milestone. |
| Simple ten-minute cycle and `T` debug control | Weather or configurable seasons arrive. |
