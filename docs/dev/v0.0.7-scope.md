# v0.0.7 scope: Advanced Horizons

**Status:** In progress - slices 1-3 complete; slice 4 load path and movement guard are in, unload lands with persistence.

**Goal:** Turn the engine into a game-ready, procedurally generated world with streaming chunks.

This page expands the [v0.0.7 roadmap entry](../roadmap.md#v007-advanced-horizons) into concrete deliverables. The roadmap remains the source of truth for milestone status and the Definition of Done.

See [v0.0.7 spec](../specs/v0.0.7.md) for the normative seed, generation, streaming, modified-chunk persistence, and `VCHK` v3 contracts.

## Definition of Done

- A new world is generated from a seed with terrain, caves, biomes, and vegetation.
- The same seed and coordinates always produce the same world.
- Chunks load and unload around the player without visible holes or hitches.
- Player edits persist, while unmodified generated chunks can be regenerated from the seed.
- The procedural world runs smoothly with the v0.0.6 lighting and water systems.

## In scope

- A world seed and deterministic noise.
- Heightmap terrain with hills, mountains, and a sea level.
- 3D caves.
- At least three biomes with distinct surface blocks.
- Deterministic trees or simple structures.
- Chunk generation on the v0.0.5 worker pool.
- Streaming chunks around the player with load and unload distances.
- Persist only modified chunks; regenerate unmodified chunks from the seed.
- Lighting, meshing, LOD, and frustum integration for generated chunks.
- CPU-only tests for deterministic generation, biome selection, cave carving, and streaming state.
- Optional experiments: SVDAG compression and GPU compute raymarching behind feature flags.

## Out of scope

- Multiplayer, networking, and servers.
- Scripting, modding APIs, and a content pipeline.
- Weather, seasons, and fluid simulation.
- Complex structures that cross chunk boundaries in the first pass.
- GPU-side world generation.

## Technical shape

### Noise and determinism

- Choose a maintained noise library and record the dependency and algorithm in [ADR-0012](../decisions/0012-fastnoise-lite-and-opensimplex2.md) before implementation. The decision selects `fastnoise-lite` 1.1.1 with OpenSimplex2.
- Seed every noise function from the world seed and chunk coordinates.
- Never depend on global RNG call order; features are deterministic functions of `(seed, position)`.
- Keep generation independent of chunk load order.

### Terrain and biomes

- Combine low-frequency continentalness and erosion with a heightmap.
- Use 3D noise for caves and overhangs.
- Derive temperature and humidity from low-frequency noise and select one of three biomes:
  - plains
  - hills
  - desert
- Assign surface blocks per biome, with stone below and water at sea level.
- Place simple trees deterministically inside a chunk; defer structures that span chunks.

### Streaming

See [ADR-0013](../decisions/0013-player-centred-chunk-streaming.md) for the streaming architecture decision.

- Keep a configurable render distance in chunks, starting around 8–16. Read it from the engine
  `config.toml`; see [Configuration](configuration.md).
- Generate chunks in a ring around the player, nearest first.
- Unload chunks beyond a hysteresis margin.
- Do not let the player enter an unloaded chunk; prioritise the chunks ahead of movement.
- Save chunks marked as modified when they unload or when the world saves.
- On load, generate the chunk from the seed, then apply its saved block data.
- Initialise lighting after generation and before the chunk becomes visible.
- Feed generated chunks into the existing meshing, LOD, frustum, and upload pipeline.

### Performance

- Generation, lighting, and meshing run on workers; only GPU uploads and world mutation happen on the main thread.
- Cap generation and upload work per frame.
- Track generation time, queue depth, loaded chunks, visible chunks, vertices, and frame time.
- Ensure the player never waits on a chunk that is already visible.

### Optional rendering experiments

- SVDAG compression: store a compressed representation for far or static chunks; keep the existing mesh path as the reference.
- GPU compute raymarching: an alternate renderer behind a feature flag; it must not replace the mesh path until it is measured and proven.
- Neither experiment is required for v0.0.7 acceptance.

### Code shape

- `src/generation.rs` — noise, terrain, caves, biomes, and features.
- `src/streaming.rs` — load/unload state, queues, and player-centred chunk selection.
- `src/world.rs` — generated versus modified chunk bookkeeping and save integration.
- `src/mesher.rs` — worker jobs for generation, lighting, and meshing.
- `src/lib.rs` — world selection, seed, and frame integration.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] A new world from a seed contains terrain, caves, three biomes, and vegetation.
- [ ] The same seed and coordinates produce identical chunks.
- [ ] Chunks load ahead of the player and unload behind them without visible holes.
- [ ] Flying or walking across chunk boundaries does not hitch.
- [ ] Edited chunks survive a restart; unmodified chunks regenerate correctly.
- [ ] Lighting and water are correct on generated chunks.
- [ ] The world holds the target frame rate at the configured render distance.
- [ ] Optional experiments are feature-gated and do not destabilise the default renderer.
- [ ] `cargo fmt --all -- --check`, `cargo clippy --all-targets --all-features -- -D warnings`, `cargo test --all-features`, `cargo doc --no-deps --all-features`, `typos`, and `mdbook build` pass.
- [ ] The README and roadmap are current and `CHANGELOG.md` has the user-visible entries.

## Deliberate simplifications

| Simplification | Revisit when |
|---|---|
| Three biomes | More biome variety becomes a content goal. |
| Trees only; no cross-chunk structures | Structures need a two-phase placement system. |
| CPU generation only | GPU generation offers a measured advantage. |
| Finite render distance with streaming | A seamless larger world becomes necessary. |
| Modified-chunk saves only | Full-world snapshots or backups are needed. |
| SVDAG and raymarching are optional | Either path is proven better than meshes. |

## Relationship to later milestones

This is the final roadmap milestone. Further work becomes its own project: multiplayer, modding, gameplay systems, or a dedicated renderer research track. The long-range terrain direction is sketched in [Terrain pipeline](../design/terrain-pipeline.md).
