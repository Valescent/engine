# v0.0.5 spec: Scale and Performance

**Status:** Scoped; implementation not started.

**Normative:** This page is the technical contract for v0.0.5. The
[roadmap](../roadmap.md) owns milestone status and the Definition of Done, and the
[v0.0.5 scope](../dev/v0.0.5-scope.md) owns deliverables and acceptance criteria. This page
owns the greedy meshing, LOD, frustum, worker, stress-scene, and metric contracts. If the scope
and this page disagree on a technical detail, this page is authoritative and the scope is
updated.

The keywords MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, and MAY are normative.

**Later milestone:** v0.0.6 extends `Vertex` to 40 bytes with packed sky/block light and AO,
and adds lighting, fog, and water. See the [v0.0.6 spec](v0.0.6.md).

## 1. Scope

This spec covers:

- Exact greedy meshing output for LOD0 and LOD1.
- The 2×2×2 LOD1 downsample and the 4/8 chunk hysteresis.
- Six-plane frustum culling against chunk AABBs.
- Asynchronous meshing jobs, revisions, priorities, and the two-mesh upload budget.
- The exact 8×8 stress fixture and its debug metrics.

Out of scope: lighting, fog, water, and transparency (v0.0.6); generated terrain and chunk
streaming (v0.0.7); occlusion culling; more than two LODs; async chunk generation; changes to
the save format or player controls. Chunk data, collision, editing, and saving remain
full-resolution.

## 2. Greedy meshing

Both LODs use one deterministic greedy mesher. LOD0 uses full-resolution cells:

- `S = 1` block per cell.
- `N = 32` cells per axis.
- Border culling uses the neighbouring chunk's full-resolution blocks.

LOD1 uses downsampled cells:

- `S = 2` blocks per cell.
- `N = 16` cells per axis.
- Boundary faces are always emitted; interior faces cull against LOD1 cells.

`Vertex` remains the v0.0.4 36-byte layout: position, normal, UV, and layer.

### 2.1 Face order and mask axes

Faces are processed in this order:

| Index | Face | Axis | Slice range | u axis | v axis | Face plane |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | +X | X | `0..N-1` | Z | Y | `s + 1` |
| 1 | -X | X | `0..N-1` | Z | Y | `s` |
| 2 | +Y | Y | `0..N-1` | X | Z | `s + 1` |
| 3 | -Y | Y | `0..N-1` | X | Z | `s` |
| 4 | +Z | Z | `0..N-1` | X | Y | `s + 1` |
| 5 | -Z | Z | `0..N-1` | X | Y | `s` |

For each face, the mask is an `N×N` grid over `(u, v)`. A cell is `Some(key)` when the
cell is solid and the face is exposed. The key is `(block, face, texture_layer)`; the face is
constant within a mask, so implementations may store `(block, layer)`.

Cell coordinates from `(s, u, v)`:

| Face axis | Cell coordinate |
|---|---|
| X | `(s, v, u)` |
| Y | `(u, s, v)` |
| Z | `(u, v, s)` |

For LOD0, the face is exposed when the neighbour in the face direction is air, including
across chunk borders. For LOD1, the face is exposed when the neighbour LOD1 cell is air; a face
on the positive or negative chunk boundary is always exposed.

### 2.2 Merge order

For each face:

1. Iterate slices `s = 0..N-1` ascending.
2. Iterate `v = 0..N-1` ascending.
3. Iterate `u = 0..N-1` ascending.
4. If the mask cell is `Some(key)` and unvisited, extend width along `u` while every cell is the
   same unvisited key. Then extend height along `v` while every cell in the width is the same
   unvisited key.
5. Emit one quad and mark the whole rectangle visited.

This is a width-first greedy merge. It produces maximal rectangles in the prescribed order; it
is not a global minimum-quad solver.

### 2.3 Quad output

For a quad at slice `s`, origin `(u0, v0)`, width `w`, and height `h`:

- Positions are in cell units, then multiplied by `S`.
- UVs are in block units: `(0,0)`, `(0,h*S)`, `(w*S,h*S)`, `(w*S,0)` in corner order.
- The shader must repeat the texture tile across the quad. The sampler MUST use repeat
  addressing in U and V, with nearest filtering and no mipmaps from v0.0.4. See
  [ADR-0007](../decisions/0007-texture-array-for-block-textures.md).
- Each vertex uses the quad's block layer.
- Triangles are `[0, 1, 2, 0, 2, 3]`.

Corners in cell units:

| Face | p0 | p1 | p2 | p3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| +X | `(s+1, v0, u0)` | `(s+1, v0+h, u0)` | `(s+1, v0+h, u0+w)` | `(s+1, v0, u0+w)` |
| -X | `(s, v0, u0+w)` | `(s, v0+h, u0+w)` | `(s, v0+h, u0)` | `(s, v0, u0)` |
| +Y | `(u0, s+1, v0)` | `(u0, s+1, v0+h)` | `(u0+w, s+1, v0+h)` | `(u0+w, s+1, v0)` |
| -Y | `(u0, s, v0+h)` | `(u0, s, v0)` | `(u0+w, s, v0)` | `(u0+w, s, v0+h)` |
| +Z | `(u0+w, v0, s+1)` | `(u0+w, v0+h, s+1)` | `(u0, v0+h, s+1)` | `(u0, v0, s+1)` |
| -Z | `(u0, v0, s)` | `(u0, v0+h, s)` | `(u0+w, v0+h, s)` | `(u0+w, v0, s)` |

The winding is counter-clockwise from outside. A single-block face must reproduce the v0.0.4
position, normal, UV, layer, winding, and vertex order exactly.

### 2.4 Mesh test vectors

Greedy output for LOD0:

| Layout | Quads | Vertices |
|---|---:|---:|
| One isolated voxel | 6 | 36 |
| Two adjacent voxels in one chunk | 6 | 36 |
| Three voxels in a line in one chunk | 6 | 36 |
| 2×2×1 slab in one chunk | 6 | 36 |
| 2×2×2 cube in one chunk | 6 | 36 |
| Two adjacent voxels across a chunk border | 10 total, 5 per chunk | 60 total, 30 per chunk |

Tests MUST compare the full `Vec<Vertex>` for the simple layouts, not only the vertex count.
The flat half of the stress fixture MUST use fewer vertices than the v0.0.4 face-culled mesher.

## 3. LOD

### 3.1 LOD1 downsample

Each LOD1 cell covers a 2×2×2 group of source blocks.

- The cell is solid when at least one source block is solid.
- Its block type is the topmost solid source block. If several share the highest Y, choose the
  lowest X, then the lowest Z.
- If no source block is solid, the cell is Air.
- LOD1 positions are cell coordinates multiplied by 2; UVs are multiplied by 2.
- The LOD1 mesh uses the same greedy mesher and face order.
- LOD1 boundary faces are always emitted. This intentionally overdraws hidden boundary faces
  to guarantee no holes at LOD0/LOD1 transitions.

LOD1 is rendering-only. Collision, editing, lighting, saving, and block queries always use
full-resolution data.

### 3.2 LOD selection

See [ADR-0010](../decisions/0010-two-level-lod-for-chunks.md) for the LOD strategy and
downsampling decision.

`Lod` has two values: `Lod0` and `Lod1`.

Distance is Euclidean centre-to-centre distance between the player's chunk and the chunk, in
chunk units. The desired LOD MUST be recalculated each frame.

- `LOD0` when distance is `<= 4.0`.
- `LOD1` when distance is `>= 8.0`.
- Distances between 4 and 8 retain the current LOD.
- Initial selection is `Lod1` when distance is `>= 8.0`, otherwise `Lod0`.

The 4/8 thresholds replace the scope's initial 8/16 values for the fixed 64-chunk stress
fixture. Revisit them if the fixture grows.

### 3.3 LOD cache

Each chunk may cache both LOD meshes:

```rust
pub(crate) struct GpuMesh {
    pub(crate) vertex_buffer: wgpu::Buffer,
    pub(crate) vertex_count: u32,
}

pub(crate) struct GpuChunk {
    pub(crate) lod0: Option<GpuMesh>,
    pub(crate) lod1: Option<GpuMesh>,
    pub(crate) active: Option<Lod>,
}
```

- Edits invalidate both cached meshes and increment the chunk revision. Invalidation schedules a
  replacement but does not remove the active buffer; the previous mesh may stay visible until a
  newer result is accepted.
- The LOD0 rebuild is urgent; LOD1 reconstruction is background work.
- When the desired LOD mesh is ready, it becomes active.
- When the desired mesh is missing, keep drawing the previous active mesh. If no mesh exists,
  draw nothing.
- LOD1 must not be written to disk or used for collision, edits, lighting, or block queries.

## 4. Frustum culling

```rust
pub(crate) struct Plane {
    pub(crate) normal: Vec3,
    pub(crate) distance: f32,
}

pub(crate) struct Frustum {
    pub(crate) planes: [Plane; 6],
}

impl Frustum {
    pub(crate) fn from_view_projection(view_projection: Mat4) -> Self;
    pub(crate) fn intersects_aabb(&self, min: Vec3, max: Vec3) -> bool;
}
```

- The six planes MUST be extracted from the camera view-projection matrix using wgpu's `0..1`
  depth convention and normalized. Plane normals point into the frustum.

| Plane | Matrix row combination |
|---|---|
| Left | `row0 + row3` |
| Right | `row3 - row0` |
| Bottom | `row1 + row3` |
| Top | `row3 - row1` |
| Near | `row2` |
| Far | `row3 - row2` |

- A chunk AABB is `[origin, origin + 32]` in world space.
- An AABB is outside when the vertex farthest in a plane's inward normal direction is behind
  that plane. It MUST be conservative: no visible AABB may be rejected.
- Culling is per frame, before drawing, and applies to the active LOD mesh.
- Empty or missing chunk meshes are not drawn.
- Occlusion culling is not implemented.
- Metrics MUST report visible chunks against total loaded chunks.

## 5. Async meshing

### 5.1 Worker pool

[ADR-0009](../decisions/0009-micropool-for-chunk-workers.md) selects micropool after running:

```console
cargo bench --bench pool_sim
```

on the reference machine and comparing rayon with micropool. When the worker code lands,
micropool moves from a development dependency to a normal dependency; rayon remains a
development dependency for `pool_sim`. The concrete pool stays behind `MeshPool`, and
`pool_sim` is not run in CI.

### 5.2 Jobs, snapshots, and revisions

```rust
pub(crate) enum Lod {
    Lod0,
    Lod1,
}

pub(crate) enum Priority {
    Urgent,
    Background,
}

pub(crate) struct MeshJob {
    pub(crate) chunk: ChunkCoord,
    pub(crate) lod: Lod,
    pub(crate) revision: u64,
    pub(crate) snapshot: ChunkSnapshot,
    pub(crate) border: BorderBlocks,
}

pub(crate) struct MeshResult {
    pub(crate) chunk: ChunkCoord,
    pub(crate) lod: Lod,
    pub(crate) revision: u64,
    pub(crate) mesh: Vec<Vertex>,
}

pub(crate) trait MeshPool: Send + Sync {
    fn submit(&self, job: MeshJob, priority: Priority);
    fn try_recv(&self) -> Option<MeshResult>;
}
```

- `MeshPool::submit` and `MeshPool::try_recv` MUST be non-blocking. The concrete pool is
  micropool, chosen by [ADR-0009](../decisions/0009-micropool-for-chunk-workers.md).
- `ChunkSnapshot` MUST own an immutable copy of the chunk's full-resolution blocks.
- `BorderBlocks` MUST provide the full-resolution one-cell border needed for LOD0 culling.
- Workers MUST NOT read `World`, touch the GPU, or mutate engine state.
- Every chunk affected by an edit increments its revision. A mesh result is accepted only when
  its revision and actual LOD still match the current requested state.
- Stale results are discarded without uploading.
- Edit rebuilds are `Urgent`; background LOD work is `Background`.
- The pending queue MUST be bounded. When full, background jobs are dropped; urgent jobs MUST
  take priority and MUST NOT block the main thread.
- The main thread MUST never wait for a worker to finish a mesh.

### 5.3 Upload scheduling

- The main thread accepts and uploads at most two completed meshes per frame.
- A result replaces only the buffer for its LOD.
- The upload budget is a count, not a time slice.
- Metrics MUST include upload time and worker rebuild time.

## 6. Stress scene and metrics

`ENGINE_STRESS_SCENE=1` selects the in-memory stress world. It MUST NOT load or modify
`world/chunks/`, and it MUST disable save on exit and `Ctrl+S`. Any other value, or no value,
uses the normal v0.0.4 world.

The fixture is exactly 64 chunks in an 8×8 grid at chunk Y `0`: chunk coordinates
`(cx, 0, cz)` for `cx, cz` in `0..=7`. Each chunk is 32×32×32.

Terrain height for a world column `(x, z)`, where `cx = x.div_euclid(32)`:

- If `cx < 4`, `h = 2`. This is the flat half.
- Otherwise, `h = 2 + (((x * 31) ^ (z * 17)) & 3)`. This is the uneven half, `h = 2..=5`.

For each column, fill `y = 0..=h`:

- `y == h`: Grass.
- `y == h - 1`: Dirt.
- `y < h - 1`: Stone.

All other cells are Air. Every chunk in the fixture is non-empty.

The measurement spawn is `(16.5, 3.0, 16.5)` on the flat half, with yaw `π/4`, pitch `0`.
The stress draw distance is 10 chunks, which contains all 64 chunks from that spawn. The target
is 60 FPS with all 64 chunks loaded at the default 1280×720 window.

Metrics are collected per frame and logged at debug once per second with the existing frame
timing:

- `frame_ms`
- `visible_chunks`
- `total_chunks`
- `visible_vertices`
- `draw_calls`
- `rebuild_ms`
- `upload_ms`

`draw_calls` counts visible non-empty chunk draws and excludes the interaction highlight.
`rebuild_ms` accumulates completed worker mesh time; `upload_ms` accumulates main-thread upload
time. The v0.0.3/v0.0.4 edit logs for dirty chunks, total vertices, and rebuild time remain.

## 7. Required tests

CPU-only tests MUST cover:

- Exact greedy output for every vector in section 2.4.
- Greedy reduction on a flat fixture compared with the naive mesher.
- LOD1 any-solid downsampling and topmost-type tie-breaks.
- LOD1 positions and UVs scaled by two.
- LOD1 boundary faces emitted regardless of neighbour solidity.
- LOD selection at 4/8 distances and both hysteresis directions.
- LOD mesh invalidation on edits.
- Frustum plane extraction and conservative AABB rejection for known inside, outside, and
  straddling boxes.
- Revision rejection for stale results.
- Queue priority, bounded-queue background dropping, and the two-upload-per-frame cap.
- The exact 8×8 stress fixture and its deterministic terrain.
- Stress mode does not read or write `world/chunks/`.
- Save/load still restores full-resolution block data.
- A frame with no completed jobs performs no uploads and does not block.

Manual GPU acceptance remains in the [v0.0.5 scope](../dev/v0.0.5-scope.md#acceptance-checklist)
and [Testing](../dev/testing.md). The scope's acceptance checklist and the roadmap's Definition
of Done remain authoritative for milestone completion.
